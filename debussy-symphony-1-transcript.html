What is included, and what is omitted?

This is a public excerpt of the single composition session, from the request to the publication step. It preserves actual recorded message and tool text, rather than replacing it with a retrospective narrative. It is not a complete internal execution log.

Private internal reasoning and provider metadata are not included. Credentials, private paths, account operations, injected tooling instructions and oversized code dumps are removed or replaced by omission markers. Redaction notes identify changes inside retained entries.

Historical claims are left as recorded, including provisional or mistaken conclusions. Later corrections remain in the transcript. Plain-text formatting is retained; old in-app links and local files may no longer be usable. Times are shown in your browser’s local time zone.
